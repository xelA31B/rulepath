# Privacy Policy — Rulepath

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

This plugin is a set of instructions (skills) that runs inside Claude. It has no servers of its own.

- **No data collection.** The plugin author does not receive, store or process your documents, descriptions or reports.
- **What Claude reads.** Within your Claude session, Claude reads the descriptions and documents you provide (for example policies or AI system registers) and uses web search to check official legal sources. Your documents are not sent anywhere by the plugin.
- **Confidentiality.** The plugin instructs Claude not to quote personal data from your documents in reports.
- **No storage, no third parties, no tracking.**

Questions: https://github.com/xelA31B/rulepath/issues
