# Document set and crosswalk

Use as a starting structure; tailor to the organisation. Verify AI Act articles against the current consolidated text.

## Frameworks

| Framework | Structure to reference |
|---|---|
| EU AI Act | Roles; prohibited practices (Art. 5); high-risk requirements (Chapter III); transparency (Art. 50); GPAI (Chapter V); AI literacy |
| NIST AI RMF 1.0 | Functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE (with categories and the AI RMF Playbook actions); Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1) for generative systems |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | Management system clauses 4 Context, 5 Leadership, 6 Planning, 7 Support, 8 Operation, 9 Performance evaluation, 10 Improvement; Annex A control areas (policies, internal organisation, resources, impact assessment, life cycle, data, information for interested parties, use of AI systems, third-party relationships) |

## AI System Register — fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| ID, name, owner | Accountable person or role |
| Purpose and users | Intended purpose; internal or customer-facing |
| Vendor / in-house | Supplier, model, version |
| AI Act role and class | Provider / deployer …; prohibited / high-risk / transparency / minimal; date assessed |
| Data used | Personal data? Special categories? Sources |
| Affected persons | Employees, customers, public |
| Human oversight | Who reviews, when, how to override |
| Risks and controls | Link to assessment |
| Status and review date | Pilot / live / retired |

## Risk & Impact Assessment — sections

1. System description and context (NIST MAP)
2. Classification under the AI Act and resulting obligations
3. Risks to health, safety, fundamental rights, and business (bias, accuracy, security, privacy, misuse)
4. Measurement and testing (NIST MEASURE): metrics, test results, limitations
5. Controls and human oversight (NIST MANAGE)
6. Residual risk, decision, approver, review date
7. Fundamental-rights impact assessment section where the AI Act requires it

## Crosswalk template

| Document / section | AI Act | NIST AI RMF | ISO/IEC 42001 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Policy — roles and accountability | Provider/deployer duties | GOVERN | Clause 5 |
| AI System Register | Registration and documentation duties (as applicable) | MAP | Clause 8, Annex A (AI system life cycle) |
| Risk & Impact Assessment | Risk management; FRIA where required | MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE | Clauses 6, 8; Annex A (impact assessment) |
| AI Literacy Plan | AI literacy duty (check current wording) | GOVERN | Clause 7 |
| Incident Procedure | Serious-incident reporting (high-risk) | MANAGE | Clause 10 |
| Transparency notices | Art. 50 | GOVERN, MANAGE | Annex A (information for interested parties) |
| Supplier checklist | Deployer due diligence | GOVERN, MAP | Annex A (third-party relationships) |
